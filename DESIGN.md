# Design System: Alghwairy Sovereign Ledger
**Project ID:** ALGHWAIRY_SOVEREIGN_2026

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The design follows a **Platinum Executive** aesthetic, characterized by an institutional, high-security, and professional atmosphere. It aims for a "Sovereign" feel—stable, reliable, and premium. The interface uses a deep obsidian and navy palette contrasted with executive gold accents.

*   **Mood:** Institutional, Secure, Stable, Premium.
*   **Density:** Balanced, with clear separation of financial data.
*   **Aesthetic Philosophy:** "Sovereign Reliability"—Offline-first focus with high-performance visuals.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles

### Core Palette
*   **Executive Navy (`#001a33`):** Primary brand color, used for the main background, sidebar, and institutional identity.
*   **Sovereign Gold (`#d4a76a`):** Secondary accent color, used for active states, primary buttons, and highlighting financial importance.
*   **Obsidian Black (`#000000`):** Used in dark theme for absolute depth and OLED optimization.
*   **Platinum White (`#f8f9fc`):** Main background color in light mode, providing a clean, airy feel.

### Semantic Roles
*   **Primary Action:** Sovereign Gold (`#d4a76a`) with high contrast text.
*   **Success:** Emerald Forest (`#276749`) for approved transactions and growth.
*   **Error:** Crimson Alert (`#c53030`) for failed audits and budget overruns.
*   **Warning:** Amber Caution (`#b7791f`) for pending approvals and low stock.
*   **Surface:** Pure White (`#ffffff`) or Deep Slate (`#0d0d0d`) for cards and modular containers.

## 3. Typography Rules
*   **Font Family:** `Tajawal` (Primary) and `Cairo` (Secondary) for a modern, elegant Arabic/English interface.
*   **Header Weight:** 900 (Heavy) for titles to project authority.
*   **Body Weight:** 400-600 for readability.
*   **Letter Spacing:** -0.5px for headers to create a dense, premium look.

## 4. Component Stylings
*   **Buttons (Executive):** Rectangular with subtle rounding (`8px`), heavy font weight (800), and a slight lift on hover (`translateY(-3px)`).
*   **Cards (Premium):** Modular containers with `12px` rounding, subtle borders, and deep shadows (`shadow-lg`). They "float" upwards on hover.
*   **Inputs (Executive):** Deep-padded (`0.75rem`), subtly rounded (`12px`), with a clear Gold focus ring.
*   **Tables (Sovereign):** Institutional layout with separated rows, heavy headers, and gold-tinted active rows.

## 5. Layout Principles
*   **Sidebar:** Fixed width (`235px`), high-contrast navy background, with a collapsed mode (`72px`) for maximizing data workspace.
*   **Header:** Translucent blurred glass effect (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`) to provide depth.
*   **Spacing Strategy:** Unified institutional spacing using a 4px/8px grid system.
*   **Compliance Banner:** High-visibility gradient shield for ZATCA and security status.
